The Sangam visual identity

One palm leaf, threaded to the next. Everything below extends that single idea: a system of records that only becomes useful when it can be bound together. The tone is a well-kept archive rather than a product launch — quiet, legible, and built to be trusted with the thing people are least willing to lose.

Quiet authority
Nothing moves, flashes or gradients. Emphasis comes from weight and space.
Rooted, not costumed
Indian reference lives in colour, script support and proportion — never in decoration.
Restraint over ornament
One accent, one rule, one dot. If an element can be removed, remove it.

Typography

DISPLAY · CHOSEN
Newsreader
Register once. Be known everywhere.
A working text serif with an optical-size axis, so the same family holds a 64 px headline and a 17 px paragraph. Sturdier than Cormorant at UI sizes, warmer than a Garamond revival, and it doesn't read as a wedding invitation.
DISPLAY · ALTERNATE
Cormorant Garamond
The most elegant option and the most fragile: hairline strokes disappear under 24 px and on low-DPI clinic monitors. Use only if headlines never drop below 36 px.
DISPLAY · ALTERNATE
EB Garamond
Bookish and dependable, with a real italic. Slightly generic in open-source circles — you will meet it in three other docs sites the same week.
BODY · CHOSEN
Mukta
One account across every clinic you visit.
संगम — एक पहचान, अनेक सेवाएँ
Devanagari and Latin drawn together by the Ek Type team, so the two scripts share weight and rhythm on a bilingual consent screen. Open apertures, low contrast, seven weights, and no personality that competes with the serif.
BODY · ALTERNATE
Hind
The same bilingual discipline with a slightly narrower Latin — better for dense tables and admin consoles, marginally less warm in running text.
BODY · ALTERNATE
Noto Sans Devanagari
The safety net: widest script coverage for a platform that will eventually need Tamil, Telugu and Bangla. Neutral to the point of anonymity — keep it as the fallback, not the voice.
MONO · CHOSEN
JetBrains Mono
Tall x-height, unmistakable 0/O and 1/l/I — the pairs that matter when someone reads an ABHA number or a client secret aloud over a phone line. Alternates: IBM Plex Mono (more formal), Space Mono (too characterful).

Space and grid

Base unit: 4 px
4 px base, 8 px rhythm. Everything below 16 px steps by 4 so icon and input metrics stay exact; everything above steps by 8 or 16 so layouts stay coarse and calm.

Grid
12 columns, 24 px gutters, 1160 px max content width, 32 px page margins (24 on tablet, 16 on phone). Auth and consent screens use a single 400 px column centred in the viewport — a decision screen gets no sidebar.
Radius — 2 px on inputs and buttons, 4 px on cards, 12 px on app icons. The mark is the only fully round shape in the system.
Borders — 1 px #E7E2D6 on cream, 1 px rgba(242,239,232,0.18) on teal. No shadows anywhere except a 2 px focus ring.

06

Ornament and illustration

Should Sangam illustrate? Mostly no.

Spot illustration is how infrastructure brands start to look like consumer apps, and an identity layer that draws smiling doctors invites the question of what else it is embellishing. The system allows exactly three graphic devices — a rule, a dot and a drop cap — all derived from the mark. Where a diagram is genuinely needed (auth flows, tenancy models), draw it as a technical figure: 1.5 px strokes, palette colours only, mono labels, no perspective, no isometric cubes.

Devanagari as decoration

सं may appear once per surface, as a watermark at 4–6% opacity behind a page header, or debossed on physical collateral. It is never a button, never an icon, never coloured. Real Devanagari in the interface is always content, never pattern — the respect shown to the script is the brand argument.
